    Present and future Koppen-Geiger climate classification maps at 1-km resolution,
    Nature Scientific Data 5, 180214 (2018). https://doi.org/10.1038/sdata.2018.214
    Data: https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.6396959 (Beck_KG_V1_present_0p083.tif)
